Welcome to the technical blog for [MimeKit](https://github.com/jstedfast/MimeKit)
and [MailKit](https://github.com/jstedfast/MailKit).

MimeKit handles MIME messages: creating, parsing, signing, and encrypting email.
MailKit provides the SMTP, IMAP, and POP3 clients for sending and receiving it.
This blog is a place to explore those libraries and the email standards behind them.

For API reference material and usage guides, visit the
[documentation](https://mimekit.net/docs/).
You can also [subscribe via RSS](/rss.xml) to follow new articles.
